FAQs

Short answers. Ask us anything else first.

Is this legal advice?

Yes, from a practising lawyer, scoped to what we discuss on the call.

Who will I speak to?

A Founders Doc lawyer who works with early-stage companies, matched to your topic.

Can I send documents first?

Yes. Send the documents and your specific queries at least 12 hours in advance.

What if I need more than a call?

Document edits, correspondence with other parties, written advice and preparatory work are outside the call unless otherwise agreed. Ask us and we will quote separately.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Let us know at least 1 Business Day in advance. Otherwise the booking is cancelled and is non-refundable.

What if I need a lawyer today?

Book an urgent consult: 15, 30 or 60 minutes, from US$80. WhatsApp is the quickest way to reach us.

How do I pay?

Card, on booking. The price you see is the price you pay.
